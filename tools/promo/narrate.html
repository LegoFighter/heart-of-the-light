Heart of the Light · narrate the making-of

Open this file in Chrome next to making-of.mp4. For each scene: read the text (edit it as you like, it is saved in the browser), press Record, the scene plays while the microphone listens, press Stop or let the scene end. Play take hears it with the picture. When all are in, Export narration.wav lays every take at its scene's start, and the command below puts the sound under the video. A take longer than its scene simply runs on into the next one.

Export

Then, in tools/promo: ./narrate.sh ~/Downloads/narration.wav or by hand: ffmpeg -i making-of.mp4 -i narration.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest making-of-voice.mp4
